Privacy Policy: Mind Lab

Last updated: 2026

Mind Lab is an educational psychology app developed by Shin. This policy explains what information the app uses.

1. Information we collect

Mind Lab does not collect, send or sell your personal information. The app does not require an account, and it does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress, bookmarks, notes, quiz results, and settings only on your device. This data stays on your device. You can clear it by removing the app's data from your phone settings, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Internet access

Lessons, quizzes and practice work offline. The app does not need internet access for its main features.

4. Children

Mind Lab is designed for general education and is written for beginners. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page, with a new date at the top.

6. Contact

For questions or feedback, use the Feedback option in the app's Settings, or contact the developer on Instagram: @shin.ali96.

7. Disclaimer

Mind Lab is for education only. It is not a diagnosis, a treatment, or a substitute for professional help. If you feel unsafe or think about harming yourself, contact local emergency services or a helpline right away.
